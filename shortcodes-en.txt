<h2 class="h-xl lao-display">Not just an <span style="color: #6a7180;">'internship'</span> programme —
but a workforce development strategy</h2>
<p class="lead">WBL is learning through real practice, <b>co-designed</b> by enterprises and vocational education institutions to build job-ready skills. Since 1981, this model has helped Switzerland become one of the world's most innovative nations.</p>
[/vc_column_text][vc_row_inner el_class="wbl-equal"][vc_column_inner pix_background_effect=""][vc_column_text el_class="panel yes"]
<h3 class="h-m"><span class="tag">WBL is</span></h3>
<ul>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>Vocational learning through real participation in production processes and services within the enterprise</li>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>Based on a vocational curriculum co-designed by enterprises and vocational education institutions</li>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>Coached by experienced instructors from both the enterprise and vocational school</li>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>Builds job-ready skills from the very first day of training</li>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>Upon completion, the company where you trained will consider hiring you immediately.</li>
 	<li><span class="ic">✓</span>A strategy for developing quality talent aligned with employer needs and the real labour market</li>
</ul>
